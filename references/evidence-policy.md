# Evidence Policy

## Evidence classes

Label candidate proof before drafting:

| Class | Meaning | Permitted use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `VERIFIED_EXPERIENCE` | The user explicitly supplied a real role, project, result, or credential | May be stated as experience, within the supplied scope |
| `PORTFOLIO_DEMO` | A demonstrable sample or prototype | Describe as a demo, sample, or prototype; never as client work |
| `TRANSFERABLE_EXPERIENCE` | Related work that shares relevant methods or constraints | Explain the connection without claiming an exact match |
| `UNVERIFIED` | Unsupported, ambiguous, or model-supplied claim | Exclude from client-facing text |

## Claim ledger

Before writing, map each planned credibility claim to:

- the exact evidence;
- its class;
- the wording boundary it permits;
- its relevance to this job.

No source means no claim. Do not infer employment, revenue, savings, client counts, certifications, platform expertise, or delivery speed.

## Honest fallback

When there is no exact precedent:

- acknowledge that the match is adjacent when useful;
- demonstrate understanding through a concrete implementation judgment;
- propose a small validation step;
- avoid defensive language and long disclaimers.

Do not write “I have not done this before” when a more precise statement is available. Prefer: “The closest relevant work is X; the shared constraint is Y.”
